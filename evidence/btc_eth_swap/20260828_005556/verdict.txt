⚠ THIS IS NOT A FAIR ATOMIC SWAP AND MUST NOT BE CALLED ONE, and the deadlines above do
  NOT change that. The claim leaf is single-key under the FUNDER's key, so the funder
  can spend without waiting and the refund is NOT an exclusive recovery path; and the
  mempool exposes the ADAPTED SIGNATURE before confirmation, so the funder — holding the
  matching pre-signature — can Ext from it and attempt a conflicting spend; if that
  confirms instead it keeps this coin and claims the other leg. Whether it wins depends
  on relay/replacement policy and miners, so the race is the claim and not a guaranteed
  theft, but a race is already enough to deny fairness. A timeout closes neither hole.
  eprint 2020/845 Sec 4.1 reasons from a signature 'published on a blockchain' and
  abstracts that race away; this does not.
⚠ THE ORDERING IS CONFIGURED, NOT ENFORCED. Each side enforces only its own deadline:
  nothing in the contract or the consensus rule checks the other leg's, and the two are
  enforced against different quantities (median time past vs block.timestamp). Pairing
  them remains a deployment decision.
