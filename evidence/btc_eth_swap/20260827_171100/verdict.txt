rules and different genesis states. This is NOT the cross-offer control the
same-venue Bitcoin runner uses — a Bitcoin transaction cannot be offered to an EVM

WHAT EACH MESSAGE BINDS — TWO DIFFERENT LISTS, NEITHER A SUPERSET OF THE OTHER.
Leg A binds chain id, contract, escrow id, payer, beneficiary and amount. Leg B's
BIP341 sighash binds its transaction inputs and prevout amounts and its outputs, and
has NO chain id. There is no transaction input on the EVM side and no chain
identifier on the Bitcoin side.

Both settlement signatures are Adapt's output. base_sign_det is used by the shim's
selftest, for the control signatures above and for the refund signatures, but neither
settlement signature comes from it. Their Adapt provenance rests on the byte-for-byte
equality of the chain-recovered signature with the retained Adapt output. Ext
succeeding on the mined sigma_2 is additional, weaker evidence: any (c, z_hat + y')
with A*y' = Y would also pass it.
